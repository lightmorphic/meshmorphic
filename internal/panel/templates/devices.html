Why this exists

This panel is only reachable from your own home network — never from the internet. But a home network also holds guests, smart televisions and anything else connected to the wi-fi. Approving devices means that being on the wi-fi is not, on its own, enough to change your website.

If you lose access to every approved device, run mm-agent devices reset on the computer itself.
