Capital letters and dashes do not matter when typing it back in. The characters used avoid anything that can be misread, so there is no letter O, no number 1 and no lowercase L.

What this is for

Your web address is worked out from a secret key held only on this computer. That is what stops anyone else — including whoever runs the entry points your site connects through — from taking your address or pretending to be your site.

The trade is that there is no password reset, because there is no account and no company holding a copy. This key is the backup.

Keeping it safe

Anyone who has this key can put their own website at your address. Treat it like a spare front door key:

  • Write it on paper. A photo in your phone’s camera roll ends up in cloud backups.
  • Keep it away from the computer itself, so one flood or theft does not take both.
  • Do not email it to yourself, and do not type it into a website.

Using it

On a new computer, install MeshMorphic and choose “restore from a recovery key”. Your address, and everything pointing at it, comes back exactly as it was.
